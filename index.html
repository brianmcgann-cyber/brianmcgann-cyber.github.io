Clarence housing market remains "highly competitive"

According to Redfin, home prices in Clarence have risen more than 15% over the past three months compared to the same period last year, with homes selling in an average of just 10 days. The median sales price now stands at $663,528.

The Clarence Football team won its home opener 33-6 against Frontier, improving to 2-0 on the season. The team travels to West Seneca West next, with kickoff Saturday at 7pm.

The sports memorabilia and apparel retailer will relocate to 2491 Wehrle Drive, with the new store expected to open in "early 2027." As part of the move, the store will no longer carry apparel such as Bills jerseys and t-shirts.

By Brian McGann · September 11, 2026

25th anniversary of 9/11: "We must never forget"

Today marks the 25th anniversary of the attacks which killed 2,977 Americans. Clarence Town Supervisor Patrick Casilio shared a statement on the anniversary. While no Clarence residents are known to have died that day, Buffalo's Beverly Eckert — whose husband was killed on 9/11 — later died in the 2009 Flight 3407 crash while traveling home to celebrate what would have been his birthday. A memorial service was held tonight at Eastern Hills Church at 7pm.

Read the Supervisor's statement →

Panthers in Clarence? Yes, and rattlesnakes too

When Clarence was first settled in 1808, early residents had to contend with wolves, panthers, mountain lions, rattlesnakes, and bears — the town even paid $5 per wolf pelt to help manage the population.

Cielito Lindo, the popular Mexican restaurant that recently closed its Transit Road location in Williamsville, will reopen in Clarence at the old El Ranchito space on Main Street. Their food truck continues operating around town in the meantime.

The annual Clarence Labor Day Fair, a tradition since 1922, kicks off today. Highlights include fireworks tonight (Friday 9/4), Nerds Gone Wild on Saturday at 7pm, the Demolition Derby Saturday and Sunday at 4pm, and Hit and Run on Sunday at 7pm.
