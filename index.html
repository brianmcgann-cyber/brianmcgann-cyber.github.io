Town continuing push for state criminal justice reforms related to juvenile theft

Clarence is leading the push for bail reform in New York related to juvenile crime, specifically car thefts in the town. Supervisor Casilio was interviewed recently on WIVB, and the interview is a good summary of the issue. The town expanded its push for changes to the state's criminal justice laws on bail and release policies just last week.

House in The Hollow nominated for National Register of Historic Places

The Brace House at 10897 Main Street in The Hollow has been nominated for the State and National Registers of Historic Places. Its exact age and history aren't fully known, but it was likely built before the War of 1812 and may have been the former Asa Ransom Tavern.

Read the full story →

Casilio discusses request to State regarding juvenile offenders

Town Supervisor Casilio spoke with Channel 7 about the town's request to New York State for changes to how the justice system handles juvenile offenders. The request follows a significant increase in car thefts in Clarence.

Read the full story →

Clarence home sells for over $2 million

6985 Mystic Court sold for $2.225 million, the second Clarence home to sell for over $2 million this year. It went for less than the $2.75 million paid earlier this year for 8971 Hillview Drive, but it remains one of the highest-priced sales in the town's history.

Costco in Amherst opening next week

The area's first Costco opens Wednesday, October 28th, at 8am. There is no free preview and the store is members-only. Costco is offering gift cards to people who sign up for a membership at the new store.
