By Brian McGann · September 4, 2026

Labor Day Fair begins today

The annual Clarence Labor Day Fair, a tradition since 1922, kicks off today. Highlights include fireworks tonight (Friday 9/4), Nerds Gone Wild on Saturday at 7pm, the Demolition Derby Saturday and Sunday at 4pm, and Hit and Run on Sunday at 7pm.

See the full schedule →

Theft at Clarence Walmart; suspect tied to three other larcenies

A Buffalo man was arrested for larceny from the Clarence Walmart, having stolen $117 worth of merchandise. Police say the man is responsible for three other larcenies this month alone.

Clarence Schools hold new teacher orientation

Over 30 new teachers will be starting in the Clarence School District this year. Last week, the school held a new teacher orientation for the incoming staff. The first day of school is Tuesday, September 8th.

9/11 ceremonies scheduled on 25th anniversary of attacks

It's hard to believe it's been 25 years since the September 11th attacks. Eastern Hills Church in Williamsville is holding a memorial service at 7pm on September 11th, in addition to other memorial events scheduled in the area.

This Week in Clarence

By Brian McGann · August 28, 2026

Clarence School Board holds monthly meeting

The Clarence Schools Board of Education held their monthly meeting on Thursday, August 26th. There were zero attendees from the public — Board President James Boglioli described it as a "quiet room." Key items included the parking lot projects underway at both the High School and Middle School, which Dr. Frahm said were "right on track" for completion by the start of the school year, and approval of the 2026-2027 school tax rates.

Watch the meeting →

Alarming car theft attempt in Harris Hill neighborhood

A brazen attempt to steal a car took place last weekend in the Harris Hill neighborhood. The thieves entered an open garage on a Sunday morning and were caught on security camera, but were not successful in stealing the vehicle. This comes after previously reported waves of car thefts in town.
